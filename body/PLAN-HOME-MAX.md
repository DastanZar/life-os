## 1. The honest abs timeline (you asked for abs + muscle)

Estimating from your phenotype (BMI 24.6 + central fat + thin limbs → ~25% body fat): fat mass ≈ 19.5 kg, lean mass ≈ 58.5 kg. Visible abs need ~12–14% BF. With +1.5 kg of newbie muscle, that's a bodyweight of **~68–69 kg**.

## 2. Weekly schedule — 5 days, not 7

**Answer to your question: 5 training days. The 2 off-days are part of the program, not a break from it.** Muscle is built during rest — training is the stimulus, sleep/rest days are when it's actually constructed. 7 hard days at an 800-kcal deficit is how you get tendinitis, a stalled bench, and a week-6 burnout. The only thing you do 7 days a week is eat protein, hit steps, and sleep.

| Day | Session | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Upper A — strength** | 50–60 min |
| Tue | **Lower A — squat bias + core** | 50–60 min |
| Wed | **Cardio Zone 2 + mobility** | 40 min |
| Thu | **Push + arms + abs** | 45–55 min |
| Fri | **Upper B — hypertrophy/back** | 50–60 min |
| Sat | **Lower B — hinge/lunge bias + cardio finisher** | 50–60 min |
| Sun | **OFF** — walk only | — |

Daily regardless: 10k steps (this is your fat-loss engine — cardio sessions are a bonus, steps are the salary).

## 3. The workouts (dumbbells + bands)

Tempo everywhere: ~2 s down, controlled up. Rest 2–3 min main lifts, 60–90 s accessories. Last set at RPE 8 (2 reps in the tank); never grind to failure on the big moves.

**MON — Upper A (strength)**
1. Flat DB bench press (floor if no bench) — 4×6–8
2. One-arm DB row (heavy) — 4×8–10/side
3. Seated DB overhead press — 3×6–8
4. Band face pull — 3×15
5. DB curl — 3×10 · Overhead DB triceps extension — 3×12

**TUE — Lower A (squat bias) + core**
1. Heavy goblet squat — 4×8–12 (3 s descent when weight caps out)
2. DB Romanian deadlift — 4×8–10
3. Reverse lunges — 3×10/leg
4. Calf raise (DB, high rep) — 4×15–20
5. Weighted plank (DB on back) 3×40 s · Lying knee raise 3×15 · Band Pallof press 3×12/side

**WED — Cardio + mobility**
40 min Zone 2: brisk outdoor walk with incline/stairs, cycling, or jump rope — conversational pace, can talk but not sing. Then 10 min hips/shoulders/ankles mobility.

**THU — Push + arms + abs**
1. Incline DB press (stack pillows/couch edge) — 3×10–12
2. DB shoulder press (light, strict) — 3×12
3. Lateral raise — 4×12–15 · Band pull-apart — 3×20
4. Hammer curl — 3×12 · Band pushdown — 3×15
5. Crunch with DB plate — 3×15 · Hanging/lying leg raise — 3×12 · Side plank w/ row 2×30 s/side

**FRI — Upper B (hypertrophy/back)**
1. Chest-supported DB row (bench/prone) — 3×12–15
2. DB pullover — 3×12
3. Rear-delt DB fly — 3×15
4. Band-assisted or negative pull-ups (see §5) — 4× as many as possible
5. Push-up ladder: 3 sets to 2 reps short of failure · Curl + lateral-raise superset — 2×12 each

**SAT — Lower B (hinge/lunge) + conditioning finisher**
1. DB single-leg RDL — 3×10/leg
2. Bulgarian split squat (rear foot on chair) — 4×10–12/leg *(the exercise that makes light dumbbells feel heavy)*
3. Goblet squat, feet-elevated heels or banded — 3×15
4. Calf raise — 3×20
5. Finisher: 5 rounds of 20 DB goblet squats + 10 band rows, 60 s rest between — light weight, get the heart rate up

**Weeks 1–2:** all sessions at ~60% effort, learn every move, film your goblet squat and RDL once for form. **Weeks 7 and 12: deload** — same exercises, half the sets. Non-negotiable.

**Progression with capped dumbbells (the home-training problem, solved):**
1. Double progression: hit top of rep range on all sets → add 1–2.5 kg
2. At max dumbbell weight: add reps (to 15–20), then slow tempo (3–4 s eccentric), then pause reps, then 1.5 reps, then unilateral variations
3. Legs will outgrow your dumbbells by week 6–8 — that's exactly why Bulgarian split squats, single-leg RDLs, and high-rep calves are built in. They keep legs progressing with ~20 kg DBs.

## 5. Abs: the truth, stated once

Abs are **made in the kitchen and trained in the gym**. Every session above includes direct ab work (progressive, weighted — abs are a muscle like any other). But the thing that reveals them is the ~10 kg of fat the deficit removes. Crunches alone would build muscle *under* the belly and change nothing visible — which is why this plan leads with compound lifts + deficit and treats ab work as the sculpting layer.

## 8. Appendix: HOME vs GYM — can home get the exact same result?

**Verdict: ~95% yes for this 13-week window, with two conditions and one honest gap.**

The science: muscle growth is driven by *mechanical tension near failure*, not by the equipment brand. Schoenfeld et al. 2017 (J Sports Med Phys Fitness meta-analysis): when weekly volume is equated and sets are taken close to failure, light loads (30–50% 1RM) produce hypertrophy statistically equivalent to heavy loads. Beginners feel this advantage most — their threshold stimulus is low. Strength (1RM) gains favor heavy/barbell work, but strength is a means, not your goal.

| Goal | Home vs Gym | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Belly fat gone | **Identical** | 100% diet + deficit driven; equipment irrelevant |
| Abs revealed | **Identical** | Kitchen + weighted ab work, both fully available at home |
| Shoulders/arms/chest/back | **Identical for 13 wks** | A beginner's pressing/row strength stays inside ~20–24 kg DBs for months; bands replicate cable tension profiles well |
| Legs | **Slight home gap** | Barbell squat/deadload progresses to 100+ kg; DBs cap out ~wk 6. Mitigated by Bulgarian split squats/single-leg RDLs — enough for aesthetic legs for a year, but absolute growth ceiling is lower |
| Max strength | Gym wins | Load-specific adaptation; irrelevant to the mirror |
| Adherence | **Home often wins** | Zero commute ≈ 3–4 h/wk reclaimed → reinvested into sleep/meal prep, which is a *recovery* advantage that partially offsets the equipment gap |

**Conditions for the 95%:** (1) doorway pull-up bar is effectively mandatory — it's the one home purchase that converts "good back" to "great back"; (2) dumbbells must adjust to ≥20 kg each (if yours cap lower, say so — the plan shifts to band-augmented loading and higher rep ranges); (3) a flat bench later unlocks fuller chest ROM, optional for this window.

**When the gym genuinely becomes worth acquiring:** month 4+ (Dec onward), when barbell overload on legs/back starts paying dividends DBs can't match. Conveniently: after your transformation window, not during it. Home now → gym at the bottleneck. Nothing built here is wasted; the split converts 1:1.
