# BODY CHART — MAX PROTOCOL (13 weeks: Sep 1 → Nov 30)
*The aggressive version of PLAN.md. Same physiology, harder execution. Assumes: full gym access, age ~25, 5–6 days/week availability. Correct me on any of the three.*

---

## 0. What "max" honestly buys you in 13 weeks

Math (computed, not vibes): BMR ~1,772 → TDEE at max activity ~2,650–2,750. Eating 1,800–1,900 → deficit ~800–900/day → **0.7–0.8 kg/week → ~9–10 kg fat lost by Nov 30**, plus 1–2 kg muscle (newbie gains). End state: **~69–70 kg, waist −10–12 cm, shoulders/back visibly built, belly down 60–70%.**

That IS a complete transformation — you'd be unrecognizable in photos and in clothes.

What max does NOT buy in 13 weeks: a full six-pack with vascularity. Starting skinny-fat at 78 kg, you're likely ~22–25% body fat; visible abs need ~12–14%. Nov 30 gets you close to the line, not across it. If you want the six-pack, the protocol extends to ~Feb. Decide now which finish line you're training for — it changes week 11–13, not week 1.

## 1. Where the real ceiling is

More is not better; **recovery is the limiter, not willpower.** The evidence-based max:
- Volume: ~10–14 hard sets per muscle per week (Schoenfeld meta-analyses; beyond ~20 sets/wk returns flatten and injury risk climbs).
- Frequency: each muscle 2–3×/week.
- Deficit: ≤1% body weight/week. Faster than that and lean-mass loss accelerates sharply (Garthe et al. 2011 RCT: 0.7%/wk losers kept ~2× the muscle of 1.4%/wk losers while losing the same fat). We sit at ~0.9% — the top of the safe band.
- The three non-negotiables that make an 800-kcal deficit muscle-sparing: **protein 170 g, sleep 7.5–8 h, every lift logged and progressing.** Break one and you're just dieting, and dieting without lifting's stimulus eats muscle.

## 2. Training — 5 days lifting + 2 cardio days + 0 skipped

**Split: Upper/Lower ×2 + Push finisher (PHAT-style hybrid), 5 sessions, 60–75 min.**

**Mon — UPPER A (strength bias)**
- Bench press 4×5–6
- Barbell row 4×6–8
- Overhead press 3×6–8
- Weighted/close-grip pulldown or assisted pull-ups 3×8–10
- Face pulls 3×15 · Dumbbell curl 2×10–12

**Tue — LOWER A (squat bias)**
- Back squat 4×5–6
- Romanian deadlift 3×8–10
- Leg press or goblet squat 3×10–12
- Leg curl 3×12 · Standing calf raise 4×10–12
- Hanging knee raise / cable crunch 3×12–15 (abs get trained like any muscle — heavy, progressive)

**Wed — Cardio + core**
- 35–40 min Zone 2 (incline walk 6–8% / bike / elliptical) + 10 min mobility

**Thu — UPPER B (volume/hypertrophy bias)**
- Incline dumbbell press 3×8–12
- Chest-supported or cable row 3×10–12
- Dumbbell shoulder press 3×10–12
- Lat pulldown (different grip) 3×10–12
- Lateral raise 3×15 · Triceps rope pushdown 3×12–15 · Rear-delt fly 2×15

**Fri — LOWER B (hinge bias)**
- Deadlift 3×5 (build carefully; never grind)
- Bulgarian split squat 3×8–10/leg
- Leg extension 3×12–15 · Leg curl 3×12
- Calf raise 4×12 · Cable crunch 3×15

**Sat — Conditioning**
- 20–25 min: 8–10 rounds of 30s hard bike/row / 90s easy (HIIT), OR 40 min Zone 2 if legs are fried from Friday. Alternate weekly.

**Sun — OFF.** Walk only. This day protects the other six.

**Weeks 1–2 are NOT max.** Load the split at ~60% effort, learn every lift, film your squat/deadlift once and check form (Jeff Nippard / Squat University). Max from week 3. Weeks 7 and 12 are **deloads** (same lifts, half the sets, same or −10% weight) — non-negotiable; deloads are what let you run hard for 13 weeks instead of quitting or getting hurt in week 8.

**Progression:** double-progression on everything (hit top of rep range on all sets → add 2.5 kg next session). Rest 2–3 min on main lifts, 60–90s accessories. Last set of each exercise at RPE 8 (2 reps in reserve); main lifts never to failure.

**Why this beats the 3-day plan for your goal:** belly fat is handled by the kitchen; the *transformation look* comes from shoulder width, upper-chest shelf, back thickness, and arm size — this split doubles the weekly volume on exactly those muscles (the ones you described as thin).

## 3. Nutrition — the numbers get tighter

| | Standard (PLAN.md) | MAX |
|---|---|---|
| Calories | 1,900–2,000 | **1,800 weekdays / 2,000 Sat-Sun refeed** (refeeds = extra carbs around training; blunts the metabolic/adaptation drift — intermittent-restriction data, Byrne 2018 *IJOS* MATADOR trial) |
| Protein | ~140 g | **165–175 g** (2.1–2.2 g/kg — the deficit is bigger, so protein protection goes up; Helms et al. 2014 natural-bodybuilder guidelines) |
| Fats | 50–60 g | 50–55 g floor (hormonal minimum) |
| Carbs | fill | fill remainder; **concentrate around training** (pre/post sessions) |
| Fiber | — | 25–35 g (satiety + vegetarian gut) |
| Water | — | 3–3.5 L (high protein + creatine) |

**Max-day vegetarian template (~1,850 kcal / 170 g):**
- Breakfast: 3 whole eggs + 2 whites scrambled, 2 toast, fruit → 32 g P
- Lunch: 1.5 cups dal + 150 g curd + 2 roti + large salad → 32 g P
- Pre-workout: banana + black coffee → 0 g P
- Post-workout: whey 1.5 scoops → 36 g P
- Dinner: 150 g paneer/tofu or 60 g dry soy chunks curry + greens + 1 roti → 38 g P
- Bed: 200 ml milk or 100 g hung curd → 10 g P
- (+ peanut/cooking-oil budget spread across the day; kitchen scale for 2 weeks, then eyeball)

**Daily floor checklist (all five, every day):** protein number · calorie number · 10k steps · 7.5 h sleep · creatine 5 g + whey + B12.

## 4. Tracking — weekly review becomes mandatory

- Daily: weight (morning, post-toilet, pre-food) → **judge weekly averages only**
- Weekly (Sunday): waist at navel relaxed, front/side/back photos, logbook review, set next week's target loads
- **Rules:** weekly avg down <0.3 kg for 2 straight weeks → cut 100 kcal (carbs). Down >1 kg/wk after week 3 → add 150 kcal (protect muscle). Strength stalling on ALL main lifts + poor sleep + cranky → that's overreaching: take an extra deload day, not an extra cardio session.
- Week 6 and week 12: optional bloodwork (lipids, B12, vit D, TSH, fasting glucose) — cheap, and it tells you what the mirror can't.

## 5. The failure modes of "max" (read twice)

1. **The week-6 crash.** Novelty dies around week 5–6; this is where max plans die. Counter: the Sunday review makes it a system, not motivation. You don't decide to train; the calendar decides.
2. **Sleep debt.** Pulling all-nighters (study/job apps) while at −850 kcal is how you lose muscle and gain belly-holding cortisol. Sleep is part of the protocol, not a lifestyle choice.
3. **Life collision.** This is 5 gym + 2 cardio + meal prep ≈ 12–14 h/week. If career chart needs crunch time, drop to PLAN.md's 3-day version for those weeks — **never drop protein or sleep to fit training in.**
4. **Ego loading.** Deadlift/bench form breaks at speed → injury → 3 weeks off → 0% transformation. The logbook's first job is to keep you honest-small.
5. **Daily scale panic.** ±1.5 kg water swings are noise. Weekly average or it didn't happen.
