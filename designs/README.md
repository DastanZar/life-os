# Design references — real app screens

Pulled from the iTunes Lookup API (Apple's own marketing screenshots — the same
screens Mobbin indexes behind its login wall). Sourced 2026-09-07.

Mobbin itself is account-gated; these App Store shots + the app descriptions
were used as the pattern source instead.

## What each app taught the Coach tab redesign

### Zing Coach (`zing-*.png`) — the check-in loop
- "Checks in daily about your energy levels, mood, and sleep, adjusting the plan in real-time"
- **Taken:** the Daily check-in mood row (Low/OK/Good/Great) at the top of Coach.
  One tap → instant coaching response in the thread, keyed to training phase
  (deload vs. training day vs. rest day) — never judgmental.

### Gentler Streak (`gentler-*.png`) — readiness framing
- "Personalized guidance that adapts to your daily capabilities, not the other way around"
- Emotional, non-guilt framing: the app meets you where you are.
- **Taken:** the mood-pill phase label ("Deload week", "Building") and the
  adherence-neutral response tone. Low day = scale the session, not "you failed."

### Cal AI (`cal-*.png`) — scan-first minimalism
- Three-step pitch: answer questions → snap photo → breakdown. Zero dashboard clutter.
- **Taken:** confidence/confirmation flow in the food scanner already follows this;
  chat-first Coach replaces the old stat-wall with one conversation surface.

### MacroFactor (`macrofactor-*.png`) — coach algorithm, no shame
- "Adapts to changes in your metabolism so you never plateau" — dynamic coaching,
  adherence-neutral language throughout.
- **Taken:** coach sub-line shows week/phase/day as *state*, not *score*.

### Hevy (`hevy-*.png`) — speed of logging
- "Most intuitive workout tracker" — every log action ≤2 taps, no confirmation dialogs.
- **Taken:** prompt chips one-tap-send; composer always visible; send swaps to mic when empty.

### Cronometer / MFP (`cronometer-*.png`, `mfp-*.png`) — density + insight
- Cronometer: 95+ micronutrients, science-backed insight cards.
- MFP (2026): built-in AI nutrition Coach with personalized guidance from history.
- **Taken:** quick-stats row (Protein/Kcal/Sleep/Streak) as glanceable context above the chat.
